# Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 9, 2026

## Scope

This is a personal, single-user application operated locally by its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not accept registrations from other users.

## Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, the application may retrieve read-only fantasy information associated with the owner's own fantasy leagues and teams. The application does not request the owner's Yahoo password.

## Processing and retention

Yahoo Fantasy information is processed locally and only as needed to provide the personal draft-assistance features. The application is designed not to persist, cache, or index Yahoo Fantasy information. Development and testing use synthetic data rather than real Yahoo information.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally and are not intentionally transmitted to third parties. They are excluded from source control.

## Sharing and sale

Yahoo Fantasy information is not sold, shared, published, used for advertising, or made available to other users.

## AI systems

Yahoo Fantasy information is not intentionally submitted to external generative-AI systems or used to train, improve, or ground machine-learning models.

## Deletion

The owner may revoke the application's access through Yahoo account settings and remove locally stored credentials. Any Yahoo information inadvertently persisted should be deleted promptly.

## Contact

Questions may be submitted through this repository's GitHub issue tracker without including credentials, tokens, or private fantasy information.

Fantasy data provided by [Yahoo Fantasy](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/).
